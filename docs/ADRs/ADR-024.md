# ADR-024 — Consolidate bounded contexts: ten fold to six (amends ADR-010)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-03 (ratified with Michael)

## Context

ADR-010 drew nine bounded contexts (ten with Housekeeping) prospectively, before most of the product existed. The app is now fleshed out, and a review of the boundaries as actually exercised (2026-08-03) found the discipline paying off unevenly:

**What the boundaries earn.** Aggregate invariants and the one-per-transaction rule caught real bugs (the duplicate-invite race, the cook-flow transaction confusion behind ADR-014). Intake's `StagedProduct` staging and Deals' Flipp ACL keep untrusted input out of the trusted model. The machine-checkable Gate 2 rules give the agentic pipeline (ADR-018) a crisp, testable contract.

**What they cost.** The dominant integration mechanism is not events (the dispatcher machinery has exactly one registered handler, which logs) but ~60 synchronous port/adapter pairs in `Plantry.Composition`, most of them pure translation boilerplate: each consuming context defines its *own* reader port and its *own* DTO for identical reads. Catalog's `Product` alone is re-projected as six DTO shapes for Recipes and at least four more across pages. Page models became the de facto composition layer — `MealPlan/Index.cshtml.cs` injects ~24 dependencies across six contexts; Intake's review page comments on its "already 18-dep" constructor. ADR-021 already conceded that write-side ports make read composition pathological (the 6.09 s week page). And several contexts are too small to be contexts: Pricing is one aggregate (`PriceObservation`, 17 files); Housekeeping is read-only detectors over other contexts' data plus a single `Dismissal` aggregate — a read model wearing a context costume.

Each micro-context carries a full boundary tax: its own DbContext, migrations, adapter folder, port interfaces, and Gate-2 surface area — paid whether or not the boundary protects an invariant.

## Decision

**The context map consolidates from ten contexts to six.** Boundaries are kept where they protect real invariants or stage untrusted input, and removed where they only generate translation ceremony.

| Target context | Composed of | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Pantry** | Catalog + Inventory | "What exists" and "how much we have" are joined on nearly every page; Inventory carries 7 adapters just to read Catalog. One context ends the product+stock stitch that dominates read-side ceremony. |
| **Market** | Pricing + Deals | One "what stores charge" context: prospective offers (deals) and observed facts (price observations) share consumers and a lifecycle — a confirmed deal *becomes* a price observation. That confirmation is today a cross-boundary write; merged, it is an intra-context state transition. The Flipp `IFlyerSource` ACL and deal-review staging remain intact inside Market. |
| **Planning** | Meal Planning + Shopping | Shopping lists are substantially derived from the plan (`ShopForWeek`, plan-fulfillment contributions, `ShoppingListItemContribution`); the pair is among the most entangled in the adapter census, and the writes express one user intention — plan the week and shop for it. |
| **Recipes** | unchanged | Large, real invariants (cook events, consume/produce lines, expansion), distinct lifecycle. Its cook flow writing Pantry stock is a legitimate cross-context saga worth keeping explicit. |
| **Intake** | unchanged | The AI/receipt ACL; `StagedProduct` staging is the pattern working exactly as designed. Commits into Pantry and Market, same as today, against fewer targets. |
| **Identity & Access** | unchanged | Generic subdomain, ASP.NET-coupled, low-friction. |
| *(dissolved)* | Housekeeping | Not a domain. Its detectors become read models in the composition read layer (ADR-021 shape); `Dismissal` — the only state it owns — moves with them. |

### Rules that survive the merges unchanged

1. **Merging contexts removes translation ceremony, not consistency rules.** Aggregate boundaries inside a merged context do not relax: `Product` and `ProductStock` remain separate aggregates under the one-aggregate-per-transaction rule; any co-commit still needs a named ADR-010 exception. A context merge is a *module* merge, not an invariant merge.
2. **Cross-context writes stay eventual (ADR-014).** The surviving write seams are few and each maps to a nameable business moment: Intake commit → Pantry + Market; Recipes cook → Pantry (consume/produce, leftover product); Recipes/Planning → shopping-list writes. Gate 2 continues to govern them.
3. **Physical schemas do not move on day one.** A merged context's single DbContext maps the existing schemas (e.g. Market's DbContext maps `pricing.*` and `deals.*`). RLS policies are per-table and unaffected. Schema renames/moves are optional later cleanup, not a merge prerequisite — this keeps each merge a code-and-DI change, not a data migration.
4. **ID-only references and the ACL stance are unchanged** for everything still cross-context.

### Read-side companion (broadens ADR-021)

ADR-021 sanctioned cross-schema read models as a *deliberate exception on demonstrated hot paths*. This ADR promotes them to the **default for multi-context page composition**: pages read through purpose-built, read-only, RLS-tested read models in the composition layer, and the per-context *reader* ports and their adapters are retired as read models take over their call sites. ADR-021's four rules (SQL fetches, C# keeps the math; read-only one-directional; lives in the composition root; guarded by contract + RLS tests) govern every such read model unchanged — what changes is only the default. Write-side ports are untouched.

### Phasing

- **Phase A (low risk, proves the direction):** Market merge; Housekeeping dissolution; begin the read-layer expansion on the worst pages (MealPlan, Recipes/Details, Cook).
- **Checkpoint:** re-evaluate. The read layer may relieve enough pain that the remaining merges get cheaper or less urgent — decide with evidence, not by momentum.
- **Phase B:** Planning merge.
- **Phase C (largest, last):** Pantry merge — touches the most-referenced context; downstream domain code barely changes (references are already bare Guids), but infrastructure, DI, boundary tests, and Gate 2 all need coordinated updates.
- **Alongside Phase A:** decide use-or-delete for the domain-event dispatcher machinery (one logging handler today); carrying it unused adds surface for agents to misuse.

### Rejected alternatives

**Pricing folds into Catalog.** Superficially "a price is a fact about a product," but the shapes disagree: Catalog is stable, trusted, low-churn reference data upstream of everything; `PriceObservation` is an append-only time series of market facts. It would also route Deals — an untrusted-source context — into writing Catalog, muddying exactly the "Catalog stays clean upstream reference data" property that keeps the rest of the system simple. Deals and Pricing, by contrast, answer the same question at different tenses.

**Wholesale merge (one context, classic monolith).** Throws away the parts that demonstrably work: the Intake/Deals ACLs, the explicit cook/commit sagas, and the machine-checkable boundary contract the agentic pipeline reviews against. The problem is boundary *count* and read-side ceremony, not boundaries per se.

**Status quo + read layer only.** Considered as a stopping point, and the phasing deliberately front-loads the read layer so this remains a live fallback at the checkpoint. But it leaves the micro-context tax (Pricing, Housekeeping) and the deal→observation boundary crossing in place for no protective benefit, and ten contexts' worth of Gate-2/adapters/DbContext surface for agents to navigate.

## Consequences

- **Most of `Plantry.Composition` evaporates over the phases**: all Inventory→Catalog adapters, Deals→Pricing, MealPlanning↔Shopping, and the 7 Housekeeping detectors go with the merges; most remaining *reader* adapters retire into read models. Write-side adapters for the surviving seams stay.
- **DbContexts drop from ten to six**; migration sets consolidate per merged context (existing applied migrations stay as history).
- **Gate 2 gets sharper, not looser**: fewer boundaries, but every one that remains protects a genuine invariant or an untrusted-input seam. `.claude/review-criteria.md`, the NetArchTest boundary tests, and ARCHITECTURE.md's context map are updated per phase — the boundary tests are the merge's definition of done, not an afterthought.
- **ADR-010's nine-context map is superseded by the table above**; its aggregate rules, ID-only references, ACL stance, and named exceptions all carry forward unchanged. ADR-014 and ADR-021 are unaffected except for ADR-021's default broadening above.
- **Named exceptions inherited intact**: the cook-anchor co-commit (ADR-010 · 2026-07-25) and Intake commit orchestration remain the sanctioned exceptions, now expressed against the six-context map.

## Relationship to other decisions

Amends **ADR-010** (context map; everything else carries forward) and broadens **ADR-021** (read models become the composition default, same four rules). Operates within **ADR-014** (surviving cross-context writes stay eventual). Serves **ADR-018**: fewer, sharper boundaries make the machine-checkable contract cheaper for agents to hold. Tracked in the `bd` epic opened with this ADR.
